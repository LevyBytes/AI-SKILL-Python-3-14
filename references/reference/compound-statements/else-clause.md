# 8.4.3. `else` clause

### 8.4.3. `else` clause

The optional `else` clause runs when control flow exits the `try` suite, no exception was raised, and no [`return`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/reference/simple_stmts.html#return), [`continue`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/reference/simple_stmts.html#continue), or [`break`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/reference/simple_stmts.html#break) statement ran. The preceding `except` clauses do not catch exceptions arising in the `else` clause.
