# 6.2.10.1. Generator-iterator methods

#### 6.2.10.1. Generator-iterator methods

The methods belonging to a generator iterator are covered in this subsection. They give you a way to drive how a generator function runs.

Keep in mind that invoking any of the generator methods listed here while the generator is mid-execution raises a [`ValueError`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/exceptions.html#ValueError) exception.
