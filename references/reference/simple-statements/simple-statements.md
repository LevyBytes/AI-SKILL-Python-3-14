# 7. Simple statements

### 7. Simple statements

A simple statement fits entirely inside one logical line. You can place multiple simple statements on the same line by separating them with semicolons. Simple statements follow this grammar:

```text

simple_stmt: expression_stmt
             | assert_stmt
             | assignment_stmt
             | augmented_assignment_stmt
             | annotated_assignment_stmt
             | pass_stmt
             | del_stmt
             | return_stmt
             | yield_stmt
             | raise_stmt
             | break_stmt
             | continue_stmt
             | import_stmt
             | future_stmt
             | global_stmt
             | nonlocal_stmt
             | type_stmt
```
