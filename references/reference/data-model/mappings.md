# 3.2.7. Mappings

### 3.2.7. Mappings

These stand for finite collections of objects indexed by arbitrary index sets. The subscript notation `a[k]` picks out from the mapping `a` the item indexed by `k`; this works within expressions and on the left of assignments or of [`del`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/reference/simple_stmts.html#del) statements. The built-in function [`len()`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/functions.html#len) gives back how many items a mapping holds.
