# 3.2.4.3. `numbers.Complex` (`complex`)

#### 3.2.4.3. [`numbers.Complex`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/numbers.html#numbers.Complex) ([`complex`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/functions.html#complex))

These stand for complex numbers held as a duo of machine-level double precision floating-point numbers. Every caveat noted for floating-point numbers carries over here. To read off the real and imaginary halves of a complex number `z`, use the read-only attributes `z.real` and `z.imag`.
