# math - Constants

### Constants

 math.pi

The mathematical constant *π* = 3.141592…, to available precision.

 math.e

The mathematical constant *e* = 2.718281…, to available precision.

 math.tau

The mathematical constant *τ* = 6.283185…, at the precision available. Tau, a circle constant, equals 2*π* — the proportion of a circle's circumference to its radius. For more on Tau, see Vi Hart's video [Pi is (still) Wrong](https://vimeo.com/147792667), then mark [Tau day](https://tauday.com/) by consuming double the pie!

Added in version 3.6.

 math.inf

A floating-point positive infinity. (Reach for `-math.inf` to get negative infinity.) It matches what `float('inf')` produces.

Added in version 3.5.
