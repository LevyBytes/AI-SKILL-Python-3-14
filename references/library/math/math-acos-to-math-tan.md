# math — Trigonometric functions

### Trigonometric functions

 math.acos(*x*)

Give back the arc cosine of *x*, in radians. The outcome falls between `0` and `pi`.

 math.asin(*x*)

Give back the arc sine of *x*, in radians. The outcome falls between `-pi/2` and `pi/2`.

 math.atan(*x*)

Give back the arc tangent of *x*, in radians. The outcome falls between `-pi/2` and `pi/2`.

 math.atan2(*y*, *x*)

Give back `atan(y / x)`, in radians. The outcome falls between `-pi` and `pi`. The vector in the plane running from the origin to point `(x, y)` forms this angle with the positive X axis. The value of `atan2()` is that, knowing the signs of both inputs, it can settle the correct quadrant for the angle. As an example, `atan(1)` and `atan2(1, 1)` both come to `pi/4`, while `atan2(-1, -1)` comes to `-3*pi/4`.

 math.cos(*x*)

Give back the cosine of *x* radians.

 math.sin(*x*)

Give back the sine of *x* radians.

 math.tan(*x*)

Give back the tangent of *x* radians.
