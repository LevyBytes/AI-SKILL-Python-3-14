# math — Hyperbolic functions

### Hyperbolic functions

[Hyperbolic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_functions) play the role of trigonometric functions but are grounded in hyperbolas rather than circles.

 math.acosh(*x*)

Give back the inverse hyperbolic cosine of *x*.

 math.asinh(*x*)

Give back the inverse hyperbolic sine of *x*.

 math.atanh(*x*)

Give back the inverse hyperbolic tangent of *x*.

 math.cosh(*x*)

Give back the hyperbolic cosine of *x*.

 math.sinh(*x*)

Give back the hyperbolic sine of *x*.

 math.tanh(*x*)

Give back the hyperbolic tangent of *x*.
