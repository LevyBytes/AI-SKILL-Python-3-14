# sysconfig - Home scheme

### Home scheme

The premise of the “home scheme” is that you assemble and look after your own private collection of Python modules. Its name traces back to the notion of a Unix “home” directory, given that a Unix user often arranges their home directory to resemble the layout of `/usr/` or `/usr/local/`. Anyone may adopt this scheme, no matter which operating system they are installing for.
