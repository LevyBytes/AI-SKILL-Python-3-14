# datetime - Constants

### Constants

These constants are exported by the `datetime` module:

 datetime.MINYEAR

The lowest year number permitted in a `date` or `datetime` object. `MINYEAR` is 1.

 datetime.MAXYEAR

The highest year number permitted in a `date` or `datetime` object. `MAXYEAR` is 9999.

 datetime.UTC

Alias for the UTC time zone singleton `datetime.timezone.utc`.

Added in version 3.11.
