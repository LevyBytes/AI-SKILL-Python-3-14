# tkinter.ttk - Combobox

### Combobox

The `ttk.Combobox` widget pairs a text field with a drop-down list of values. It is a subclass of `Entry`.

On top of the methods picked up from `Widget`: `Widget.cget()`, `Widget.configure()`, `Widget.identify()`, `Widget.instate()` and `Widget.state()`, plus these picked up from `Entry`: `Entry.bbox()`, `Entry.delete()`, `Entry.icursor()`, `Entry.index()`, `Entry.insert()`, `Entry.selection()`, `Entry.xview()`, it carries a few more methods, covered at `ttk.Combobox`.
