# tkinter.ttk — Sizegrip

### Sizegrip

The `ttk.Sizegrip` widget (also called a grow box) lets the user resize the toplevel window it sits in by pressing the grip and dragging.

Beyond what it inherits from `ttk.Widget`, this widget has neither specific options nor specific methods.
