# statistics - Statistics for relations between two inputs

### Statistics for relations between two inputs

The functions here compute statistics describing the relationship between a pair of inputs.

| `covariance()` | Sample covariance for two variables. |
| --- | --- |
| `correlation()` | Pearson and Spearman’s correlation coefficients. |
| `linear_regression()` | Slope and intercept for simple linear regression. |
