### Internationalization

The modules covered in this chapter assist you in building software that stays independent of language and locale, offering ways to pick which language program messages appear in or to shape output so it follows local conventions.

Here are the modules covered in this chapter:

- [`gettext` — Multilingual internationalization services](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/gettext.html)
  - [GNU **gettext** API](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/gettext.html#gnu-gettext-api)
  - [Class-based API](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/gettext.html#class-based-api)
    - [The `NullTranslations` class](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/gettext.html#the-nulltranslations-class)
    - [The `GNUTranslations` class](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/gettext.html#the-gnutranslations-class)
    - [Solaris message catalog support](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/gettext.html#solaris-message-catalog-support)
    - [The Catalog constructor](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/gettext.html#the-catalog-constructor)

  - [Internationalizing your programs and modules](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/gettext.html#internationalizing-your-programs-and-modules)
    - [Localizing your module](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/gettext.html#localizing-your-module)
    - [Localizing your application](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/gettext.html#localizing-your-application)
    - [Changing languages on the fly](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/gettext.html#changing-languages-on-the-fly)
    - [Deferred translations](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/gettext.html#deferred-translations)

  - [Acknowledgements](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/gettext.html#acknowledgements)

- [`locale` — Internationalization services](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/locale.html)
  - [Background, details, hints, tips and caveats](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/locale.html#background-details-hints-tips-and-caveats)
  - [Locale names](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/locale.html#locale-names)
  - [For extension writers and programs that embed Python](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/locale.html#for-extension-writers-and-programs-that-embed-python)
  - [Access to message catalogs](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/locale.html#access-to-message-catalogs)
