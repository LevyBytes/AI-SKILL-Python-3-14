### `curses.panel` - A panel stack extension for curses

---

Think of a panel as a window that also carries a notion of depth. Because of that depth ordering, several panels may sit layered over one another, and the screen only shows whatever part of each window isn't hidden by something above it. You can insert panels, raise or lower them within the ordering, and take them out again.
