# tkinter - Tkinter Life Preserver

### Tkinter Life Preserver

This section isn’t meant to serve as a complete tutorial on Tk or on Tkinter. For something like that, turn to one of the external resources mentioned earlier. What it offers instead is a rapid orientation: what a Tkinter application looks like, which foundational Tk concepts matter, and how the Tkinter wrapper is put together.

What follows in this section will help you pin down the classes, methods, and options your Tkinter application requires, and point you toward fuller documentation on them, the official Tcl/Tk reference manual included.
