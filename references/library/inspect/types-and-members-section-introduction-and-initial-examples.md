# inspect — Types and members — Section introduction and initial examples

### Types and members

To collect the members of an object such as a module or a class, call the `getmembers()` function. Functions prefixed with "is" exist chiefly as handy options to hand in as the second argument to `getmembers()`. They likewise tell you when the special attributes below are likely to be present (consult [Import-related attributes on module objects](https://docs.python.org/3.14/reference/datamodel.html#import-mod-attrs) for the attributes carried by modules):
