# decimal — Logical operands

### Logical operands

The `logical_and()`, `logical_invert()`, `logical_or()`, and `logical_xor()` methods require that their arguments be *logical operands*. A *logical operand* is any `Decimal` instance whose exponent and sign are each zero and whose digits are every one of them either `0` or `1`.
