# numbers: `numbers.Integral`

*class *numbers.Integral

Extends `Rational` and throws in a conversion to [`int`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/functions.html#int). It hands you defaults for [`float()`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/functions.html#float), `numerator`, and `denominator`, plus abstract methods covering modular [`pow()`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/functions.html#pow) and the bit-string operators `<<`, `>>`, `&`, `^`, `|`, `~`.
