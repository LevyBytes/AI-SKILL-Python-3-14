# numbers: `numbers.Complex`

### The numeric tower

 *class *numbers.Complex

Anything subclassing this type stands for complex numbers and carries the operations defined on the built-in [`complex`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/functions.html#complex) type. Those operations are: conversions to `complex` and [`bool`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/functions.html#bool), `real`, `imag`, `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `**`, [`abs()`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/functions.html#abs), `conjugate()`, `==`, and `!=`. Every one of them apart from `-` and `!=` is abstract.
