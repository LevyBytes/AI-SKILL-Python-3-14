# numbers: `numbers.Real`

*class *numbers.Real

On top of `Complex`, the `Real` class brings in operations meant for real numbers.

Briefly, the additions are: a conversion to [`float`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/functions.html#float), [`math.trunc()`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/math.html#math.trunc), [`round()`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/functions.html#round), [`math.floor()`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/math.html#math.floor), [`math.ceil()`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/math.html#math.ceil), [`divmod()`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/functions.html#divmod), `//`, `%`, `<`, `<=`, `>`, and `>=`.

Real supplies defaults as well for [`complex()`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/functions.html#complex), `real`, `imag`, and `conjugate()`.
