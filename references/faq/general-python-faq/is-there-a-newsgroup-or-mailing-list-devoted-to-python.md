# Is there a newsgroup or mailing list devoted to Python?

### Is there a newsgroup or mailing list devoted to Python?

A newsgroup exists, *comp.lang.python*, alongside a mailing list, [python-list](https://mail.python.org/mailman/listinfo/python-list). Because the two are gatewayed together, anyone who reads the news has no need to join the mailing list. With hundreds of messages arriving daily, *comp.lang.python* sees heavy traffic, and Usenet readers tend to handle that load more comfortably.

You can catch announcements of fresh software releases and events on comp.lang.python.announce, a moderated low-traffic list running roughly five messages a day. It's reachable as [the python-announce mailing list](https://mail.python.org/mailman3/lists/python-announce-list.python.org/).

Additional details on other mailing lists and newsgroups live at [https://www.python.org/community/lists/](https://www.python.org/community/lists/).
