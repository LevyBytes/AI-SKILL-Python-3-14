### Extending/Embedding FAQ

### Can I create my own functions in C?

Indeed: built-in modules holding functions, variables, exceptions, and even brand-new types can be authored in C. The [Extending and Embedding the Python Interpreter](https://docs.python.org/3.14/extending/index.html#extending-index) document walks through how.

The subject also tends to appear in the bulk of mid-level and advanced Python texts.
