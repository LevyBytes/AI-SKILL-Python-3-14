### Introduction

Python’s 2.0 release shipped on October 16, 2000. The pages that follow walk through the notable additions in 2.0, call attention to several other handy adjustments, and flag a handful of breaking changes that could force you to revise existing code.

Between any two releases, work on Python carries on without pause, and a continual stream of corrections and enhancements keeps arriving. Plenty of small repairs, a couple of speedups, more docstrings, and clearer error reporting all made their way into 2.0; enumerating every one would be hopeless, yet collectively they matter a great deal. If you’d like the complete inventory, the publicly available CVS logs are there to browse. Much of this momentum traces to the five PythonLabs developers who are now compensated to devote their workdays to squashing bugs, plus the better communication that followed the migration to SourceForge.
