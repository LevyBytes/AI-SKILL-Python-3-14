### Introduction

The pages that follow walk through what arrived in Python 2.2.2, which shipped on October 14, 2002. That build was a bugfix release of Python 2.2, whose first appearance dates to December 21, 2001.

It’s fair to label Python 2.2 the “cleanup release”. A handful of additions — generators and iterators among them — are genuinely brand-new, yet the bulk of the work, sweeping as it sometimes is, targets the tidying of rough edges and shadowy corners left over from the language’s design.

Rather than serving as an exhaustive specification of every addition, this write-up aims to give you a handy survey. To get the whole story, consult the Python 2.2 documentation, including the [Python Library Reference](https://docs.python.org/2.2/lib/lib.html) and the [Python Reference Manual](https://docs.python.org/2.2/ref/ref.html). When you’re after the entire implementation along with the reasoning behind a given change, turn to the PEP that covers that particular addition.
