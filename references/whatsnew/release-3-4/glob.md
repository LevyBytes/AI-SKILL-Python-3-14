# What's New In Python 3.4 / glob — glob

### glob

A new function, [`escape()`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/glob.html#glob.escape), offers a route to escape special characters within a filename so that they stay out of the globbing expansion and are matched literally instead. (Contributed by Serhiy Storchaka in [bpo-8402](https://bugs.python.org/issue?@action=redirect&bpo=8402).)
