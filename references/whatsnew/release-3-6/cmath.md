# What's New In Python 3.6 / cmath — cmath, math

### cmath

A fresh [`cmath.tau`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/cmath.html#cmath.tau) (*τ*) constant now exists. (Contributed by Lisa Roach in [bpo-12345](https://bugs.python.org/issue?@action=redirect&bpo=12345), see [**PEP 628**](https://peps.python.org/pep-0628/) for details.)

Fresh constants: [`cmath.inf`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/cmath.html#cmath.inf) plus [`cmath.nan`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/cmath.html#cmath.nan) mirroring [`math.inf`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/math.html#math.inf) and [`math.nan`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/math.html#math.nan), together with [`cmath.infj`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/cmath.html#cmath.infj) and [`cmath.nanj`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/cmath.html#cmath.nanj) following the layout that complex repr uses. (Contributed by Mark Dickinson in [bpo-23229](https://bugs.python.org/issue?@action=redirect&bpo=23229).)
