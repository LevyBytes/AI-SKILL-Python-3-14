# What's New In Python 3.6 / rlcompleter

### rlcompleter

Unless the prefix begins with underscores, private and special attribute names are now left out. After certain completed keywords, either a space or a colon gets appended. (Contributed by Serhiy Storchaka in [bpo-25011](https://bugs.python.org/issue?@action=redirect&bpo=25011) and [bpo-25209](https://bugs.python.org/issue?@action=redirect&bpo=25209).)
