# What’s New In Python 3.12 / math — math

### math

- Add [`math.sumprod()`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/math.html#math.sumprod) that works out a sum of products. (Contributed by Raymond Hettinger in [gh-100485](https://github.com/python/cpython/issues/100485).)

- Broaden [`math.nextafter()`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/math.html#math.nextafter) with a *steps* argument so it can shift up or down by several steps in one call. (Contributed by Matthias Goergens, Mark Dickinson, and Raymond Hettinger in [gh-94906](https://github.com/python/cpython/issues/94906).)
