# Python 3.9.0 alpha 1 / library / lib2to3 understands expressions placed after seen f or

[bpo-33348](https://bugs.python.org/issue?@action=redirect&bpo=33348): lib2to3 now understands expressions placed after `*` and `**`, as seen in `f(*[] or [])`.
