### mmap

To reach the `madvise()` system call, the [`mmap.mmap`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/mmap.html#mmap.mmap) class now exposes an [`madvise()`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/mmap.html#mmap.mmap.madvise) method.
