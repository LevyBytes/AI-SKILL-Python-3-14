### PEP 563 may not be the future

[**PEP 563**](https://peps.python.org/pep-0563/) Postponed Evaluation of Annotations — the `from __future__ import annotations` [future statement](https://docs.python.org/3.14/reference/simple_stmts.html#future) once slated to ship in Python 3.10 — has been shelved with no fixed timeline. For background, read [this message from the Steering Council](https://mail.python.org/archives/list/python-dev@python.org/message/VIZEBX5EYMSYIJNDBF6DMUMZOCWHARSO/).
