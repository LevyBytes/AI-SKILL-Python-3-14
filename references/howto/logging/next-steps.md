### Next Steps

With that the basic tutorial wraps up. It ought to be sufficient to get you started and running with logging. The logging package has plenty more on offer, but to extract its full value you'll have to put a bit more of your time into reading the sections that follow. If you feel ready for that, pour yourself some of your preferred beverage and press on.

When your logging requirements are modest, lean on the examples above to fold logging into your own scripts; and should you hit trouble or fail to grasp something, do post a question in the Help category of the [Python discussion forum](https://discuss.python.org/c/help/7), where help should reach you before long.

Still around? Feel free to keep reading the next several sections, which deliver a somewhat more advanced and in-depth tutorial than the basic one above. Once you've done that, you may wish to look over the [Logging Cookbook](https://docs.python.org/3.14/howto/logging-cookbook.html#logging-cookbook).
