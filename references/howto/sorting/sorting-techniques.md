### Sorting Techniques

Author:

Andrew Dalke and Raymond Hettinger

Every Python list ships with a [`list.sort()`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/stdtypes.html#list.sort) method that reorders its contents directly in place. Alongside it, the [`sorted()`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/functions.html#sorted) built-in function produces a brand-new ordered list out of any iterable you give it.

The pages that follow survey the assorted ways you can order and arrange data when working in Python.
