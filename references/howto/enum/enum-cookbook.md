# Enum Cookbook

### Enum Cookbook

Although [`Enum`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/enum.html#enum.Enum), [`IntEnum`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/enum.html#enum.IntEnum), [`StrEnum`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/enum.html#enum.StrEnum), [`Flag`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/enum.html#enum.Flag), and [`IntFlag`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/enum.html#enum.IntFlag) should handle most use-cases, they can’t handle every one. What follows are recipes for assorted kinds of enumerations you can use as-is, or treat as models for building your own.
