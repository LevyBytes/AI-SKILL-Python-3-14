### Subclassing EnumType

Tailoring [`Enum`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/enum.html#enum.Enum) subclasses, by way of class decorators or custom functions, handles the bulk of enum requirements; even so, you can subclass [`EnumType`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/enum.html#enum.EnumType) to deliver a distinct Enum experience.
