# Prerequisites

### Prerequisites

You need to have:

- GDB 7 or newer. (On older GDB versions, refer to `Misc/gdbinit` in the Python 3.11 sources or earlier.)

- Debugging information compatible with GDB, covering Python plus whichever extension you are debugging.

- The `python-gdb.py` extension.

Although the extension ships with Python, it may travel as a separate package or not ship at all. As examples, a few common systems get tips below. Bear in mind that even where the steps fit your system, they could be out of date.
