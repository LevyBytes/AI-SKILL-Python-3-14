# Simple patterns

### Simple patterns

Let us begin with the most basic regular expressions there are. Because regular expressions act on strings, the natural starting point is the everyday job of matching characters.

If you want a thorough treatment of the computer-science theory behind regular expressions — deterministic and non-deterministic finite automata — nearly any textbook covering compiler construction will serve.
