# History

### History

Among the assorted IPC mechanisms, sockets dominate in popularity by a wide margin. Pick any platform and you'll probably find some speedier IPC option there, yet once you need to communicate across platforms, sockets are practically the only contender.

Their birthplace was Berkeley, where they arrived as part of the BSD flavor of Unix, and they caught on explosively alongside the internet. The reason holds up: pairing sockets with INET turns reaching any machine anywhere on earth into something astonishingly straightforward — certainly next to the rival approaches.
